# How to play

[← Back to README](../README.md)

## Create

On the title choose **New world**. Pick an island (The Fading Grove, Cairnwater or Heron Reach) or type or roll a seed, choose **Empty land** (grass, trees and water, no animals) or **Wild** (rabbits, deer and foxes already live there), give it a name and choose **Create world**. **Continue** reopens your last world; **My worlds** lists every world you have made.

## Populate

The dock holds a palette of wooden pegs: **Look**, **Rabbit**, **Wolf**, **Deer**, **Fox**, **Tree**, **Grass** and **Lift out**. Choose one, then click or tap the island. A peg on the ground shows where it would go: green where it can, madder where it cannot, and a short note says why (open water, unreachable ground, nothing takes root there, the island is full). Animals set down while time is paused appear at once. **Lift out** removes an animal or something you planted.

## Observe

Use **Play**/**Pause** and **1×**, **2×**, **4×** or **8×** to set the pace. Choose **Look** and tap an animal to open its card: its name, sex and age, what it is doing and why ("Chasing Clover with the pack", "Hiding from Fern"), its food, water, energy and stamina, its pack and its litters. **Follow** keeps the camera with it. The name board counts every species on the island.

## Experiment and discover

**What happened** lists the moments the world produced (a pack forming, a first kill, an escape, a first litter, a boom, a crash, an animal that starved, a fire) with **Why?** to follow each back to its causes and **Show me** to go there. **Timeline** lets you go back to any moment and start another history beside the first, compare the two, and share a world as a code. Worlds save automatically.

## Controls

| Action | Touch (Android, iOS) | Mouse and keyboard (Desktop, Web) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Set down or select | Tap the island | Click the island |
| Orbit | Drag one finger | Drag the primary mouse button, or focus the viewport and use the arrow keys |
| Pan | Drag two fingers together | Drag the secondary mouse button, Shift-drag, or Shift and the arrow keys |
| Zoom | Pinch | Scroll, or `+` and `-` in the focused viewport |

**Focus island**, or `Home` in the focused 3D view, returns to the overview. Settings hold sound, Reduce Motion and text size; its developer section holds timeline tick tools.
